## The mark: an engraved control-panel badge

**One idea.** The house that sleeps while the station keeps watch, cut into a single engraved plate.

**Where it would be used.** A square mark at 512 px and smaller, on the navy board of the existing visuals. It must read at 64 px.

**Model and why.** Higgsfield's `brand-asset-creation` workflow (named in ART-DIRECTION.md for marks), in the Soul family per Ali's standing choice. A mark needs a clean, flat, centred object on a plain field, which is what that workflow is for; a photographic model would add lighting and perspective the board does not have. HQ to confirm the exact model id before spending.

**Style lock.** Engraved traffolyte (two-layer laminate): ivory top layer cut through to a navy core. Single-stroke engraving lines of one even width, as an engraving machine cuts them. Square plate, small radius, two brass rivets. Flat, front-on, no perspective, no drop shadow, no glow except the one window.

**Prompt (slot order: subject, material, composition, colour, detail, finish, negatives).**

> A square engraved control-panel badge, front view, centred. The plate is ivory traffolyte laminate (#ebe5d5) with the design engraved through to a deep navy core (#10202f). Engraved in single, even-width lines: a small gabled house with a chimney, two ground-floor windows and a door; one window is filled with warm signal amber (#f5a63c) as if lit at night, the only colour on the plate. From the house a single thick horizontal line runs right to a short vertical busbar with three small square breakers and three tiny round lamps. Two small brass rivets at the left and right edges of the plate. Flat orthographic view, even soft light, crisp machine-cut edges, a faint satin sheen on the laminate. Plain navy background (#10202f).
>
> Negative: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no perspective, no 3D bevel, no drop shadow, no gradient background, no glow except the window, no extra colours, no clip-art style, no cartoon, no grunge, no scratches.

**Acceptance check.** At 64 px: the house and the lit window still read; no lettering crept in; the amber is the only accent; the ivory and navy match the hex values above within a small tolerance.
